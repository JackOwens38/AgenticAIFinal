# Raclin Murphy Museum of Art — Floor Plan & Wayfinding Knowledge Base

_University of Notre Dame. Spatial model derived from the official museum plan map. Each statement below is self-contained for retrieval._

## Building overview

The Raclin Murphy Museum of Art has four levels: the Lower Level, Level 1 (the entrance level), Level 2, and Level 3. A central Grand Atrium on Level 1 rises open to a balcony on Level 2. The elevator and the North and South stairs connect all four levels.

- **Level 1** — Entrance level: Grand Atrium, Welcome Desk, Ivan's Cafe, Book Shop, Learning Commons, Teaching Gallery, African Art, and European & American Art 1700-1900.
- **Level 2** — Balcony level: European Art Through 1700, the Mary Queen of Families Chapel, Indigenous Art of the Americas (North American, Mesoamerican, Central & South American), Art of the Spanish Americas, Temporary Exhibitions, and the Founders Room.
- **Level 3** — Education level: Art Studio A and B and a flexible north space. No permanent collection galleries.
- **the Lower Level** — Lower Level: International Modern and Contemporary Art, the Sculpture Court, and the Object Study Room.

## Vertical circulation

The elevator and the North and South stairs connect all four levels (Lower Level, Level 1, Level 2, Level 3). On Level 1 they open onto the Grand Atrium; on Level 2 onto the balcony. To change floors, use the elevator (near the center of each floor) or the North or South stairs.

## Where each space is located

### Level 1

Main Entrance is located on Level 1, on the south (center) of the museum. The visitor entrance is at the south-center of Level 1 and leads directly into the Grand Atrium and Welcome Desk.
Grand Atrium is located on Level 1, on the center of the museum. The Grand Atrium is the central hall of Level 1; the North and South stairs and the elevator open onto it. It is open above to the Level 2 balcony.
Welcome Desk is located on Level 1, on the center-south (in the Grand Atrium) of the museum. The Welcome Desk sits in the Grand Atrium just inside the entrance.
Coat Room is located on Level 1, on the center-south (east of the Welcome Desk) of the museum. The Coat Room is just east of the Welcome Desk.
Ivan's Cafe is located on Level 1, on the northwest of the museum. Ivan's Cafe is in the northwest of Level 1, off the Cafe Lobby, next to the Book Shop.
Book Shop is located on Level 1, on the northwest of the museum. The Book Shop is in the northwest of Level 1, beside Ivan's Cafe.
Cafe Lobby is located on Level 1, on the north (center) of the museum. The Cafe Lobby is at the north-center of Level 1, linking the cafe/book shop to the atrium.
European and American Art 1700-1900 is located on Level 1, on the east (wrapping from northeast to southeast) of the museum. This gallery occupies the entire east side of Level 1, wrapping around the atrium from the northeast to the southeast.
Restrooms (Level 1) is located on Level 1, on the east (center, off the atrium) of the museum. Level 1 restrooms, including a Family Restroom, are on the east side off the Grand Atrium.
Learning Commons is located on Level 1, on the west of the museum. The Learning Commons is on the west side of Level 1.
Teaching Gallery is located on Level 1, on the southwest (corner) of the museum. The Teaching Gallery is in the southwest corner of Level 1, next to African Art.
African Art is located on Level 1, on the south (center-west, between the Teaching Gallery and the atrium) of the museum. African Art is on the south side of Level 1, between the Teaching Gallery (west) and the Grand Atrium.

### Level 2

Balcony is located on Level 2, on the center of the museum. The Level 2 Balcony rings the open Grand Atrium; all Level 2 spaces open off it.
Temporary Exhibitions is located on Level 2, on the west and northwest of the museum. The Temporary Exhibitions galleries occupy the west and northwest of Level 2. Rotating/current exhibitions are shown here.
Founders Room is located on Level 2, on the north (center) of the museum. The Founders Room (used for meetings) is at the north-center of Level 2, between Temporary Exhibitions and Indigenous Art.
Indigenous Art of the Americas is located on Level 2, on the northeast of the museum. Indigenous Art of the Americas is in the northeast of Level 2. Its North American sub-gallery is on the north side and its Mesoamerican sub-gallery toward the east; the Central and South American sub-gallery continues down the east side.
Central and South American (Indigenous Art) is located on Level 2, on the east of the museum. The Central and South American sub-gallery of Indigenous Art runs down the east side of Level 2, between the Mesoamerican gallery and Art of the Spanish Americas.
Art of the Spanish Americas is located on Level 2, on the southeast (corner) of the museum. Art of the Spanish Americas is in the southeast corner of Level 2, below the Central and South American gallery and next to the east wing of European Art Through 1700.
European Art Through 1700 is located on Level 2, on the south (in two wings flanking the Chapel) of the museum. European Art Through 1700 occupies the south side of Level 2 in two wings, a southwest wing and a southeast wing, that flank the Mary, Queen of Families Chapel.
Mary, Queen of Families Chapel is located on Level 2, on the south (center, between the two European Art Through 1700 wings) of the museum. The Mary, Queen of Families Chapel is at the south-center of Level 2, centered between the two wings of European Art Through 1700, directly across the balcony from the Founders Room.
Restrooms (Level 2) is located on Level 2, on the east (center, off the balcony) of the museum. Level 2 restrooms are on the east side off the balcony.

### Level 3

Art Studio A is located on Level 3, on the south of the museum. Art Studio A is on the south side of Level 3.
Art Studio B is located on Level 3, on the south (east of Studio A) of the museum. Art Studio B is on the south side of Level 3, east of Art Studio A.
Restrooms (Level 3) is located on Level 3, on the east of the museum. Level 3 restrooms are on the east side.
Level 3 Landing is located on Level 3, on the center of the museum. Level 3 is the education floor (art studios and a flexible north space); it has no permanent collection galleries.

### the Lower Level

Sculpture Court is located on the Lower Level, on the north of the museum. The Sculpture Court is at the north end of the Lower Level, adjoining the International Modern and Contemporary Art gallery.
International Modern and Contemporary Art is located on the Lower Level, on the center of the museum. The International Modern and Contemporary Art gallery is the central gallery of the Lower Level, with the Sculpture Court to its north and the Object Study Room to its south.
Object Study Room is located on the Lower Level, on the south of the museum. The Object Study Room is at the south end of the Lower Level.
Restrooms (Lower Level) is located on the Lower Level, on the center of the museum. Lower Level restrooms are in the center, off the International Modern and Contemporary Art gallery.

## What is on each floor

On Level 1 you will find: European and American Art 1700-1900; Teaching Gallery; African Art. Facilities on this floor: Main Entrance; Welcome Desk; Coat Room; Ivan's Cafe; Book Shop; Restrooms (Level 1); Learning Commons.
On Level 2 you will find: Temporary Exhibitions; Founders Room; Indigenous Art of the Americas; Central and South American (Indigenous Art); Art of the Spanish Americas; European Art Through 1700; Mary, Queen of Families Chapel. Facilities on this floor: Restrooms (Level 2).
On Level 3 you will find: no permanent collection galleries. Facilities on this floor: Art Studio A; Art Studio B; Restrooms (Level 3).
On the Lower Level you will find: Sculpture Court; International Modern and Contemporary Art; Object Study Room. Facilities on this floor: Restrooms (Lower Level).

## Finding the nearest facilities

Restrooms are on every public level: on Level 1 (east side off the Grand Atrium, including a Family Restroom), on Level 2 (east side off the balcony), on Level 3 (east side), and on the Lower Level (center). Ivan's Cafe and the Book Shop are on Level 1 in the northwest, off the Cafe Lobby. The Welcome Desk and Coat Room are in the Grand Atrium just inside the Level 1 entrance.

## Common questions

Where is the chapel? The Mary, Queen of Families Chapel is on Level 2, at the south-center, between the two wings of European Art Through 1700 and across the balcony from the Founders Room. From the Level 1 entrance, cross the Grand Atrium and take the elevator or the North or South stairs up one level to Level 2; the chapel is straight across the balcony on the south side.
Where do I enter the museum? The Main Entrance is on the south side of Level 1 and opens into the Grand Atrium and Welcome Desk.
Where is the modern and contemporary art? International Modern and Contemporary Art is on the Lower Level (one level below the entrance), reached by the elevator or stairs; the Sculpture Court adjoins it to the north.
Which floor is African Art on? African Art is on Level 1 (the entrance level), on the south-center, next to the Teaching Gallery.
Which floor is Indigenous Art of the Americas on? Level 2, in the northeast, with North American, Mesoamerican, and Central & South American sub-galleries.
Where are the art studios? Art Studio A and Art Studio B are on Level 3, the education level, on the south side; Studio B is east of Studio A. Take the elevator or stairs to Level 3.
